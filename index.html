Ride-Hailing & Parcel Delivery Guidelines

Ride-Hailing Guidelines

Allowed in the Ride

  • Small handbags and personal luggage
  • Personal electronic devices (mobile phones, laptops, etc.)
  • Approved pet carriers (only under the pet-friendly ride option)
  • Properly packed food items

Not Allowed in the Ride

  • Alcohol, drugs, or tobacco products
  • Firearms, sharp objects, or explosives
  • Illegal substances or contraband
  • Large cargo, heavy machinery, or oversized packages
  • Offensive behavior, harassment, or damage to the vehicle

Safety & Conduct Rules

  • Follow the driver’s instructions at all times
  • Keep the vehicle clean and avoid littering
  • Seat belts must be worn throughout the ride
  • Loud, disruptive, or inappropriate behavior is not permitted
  • Smoking inside the vehicle is strictly prohibited

Parcel Delivery Guidelines

Allowed for Delivery

  • Documents and letters
  • Small personal packages
  • Clothing, accessories, and non-perishable items
  • Sealed and approved food items

Prohibited for Delivery

  • Cash, gold, or valuable jewelry
  • Alcohol, drugs, or tobacco products
  • Weapons, explosives, or flammable items
  • Live animals, perishable goods, or illegal substances
  • Fragile items without proper protective packaging

Parcel Safety Rules

  • Ensure parcels are securely packed for safe transportation
  • Provide accurate pickup and drop-off information
  • Do not send restricted or prohibited items
  • The sender is fully responsible for verifying parcel contents

Important Notice

  • Any violation of these guidelines may result in penalties or account suspension
  • Hyzr reserves the right to inspect suspicious parcels for security and compliance
